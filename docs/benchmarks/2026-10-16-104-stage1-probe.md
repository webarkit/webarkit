# #104, Stage 1 probe: can `jitterPx` respond to a refused detection, and which clips can show it (2026-10-16)

**A Stage 1 probe under [the step protocol](./step-protocol.md), run on
2026-10-16 before [#104](https://github.com/webarkit/webarkit/issues/104) is
scoped. Not campaign data: no provenance, no device, the instrument untouched,
never reportable as a round.** It filters the desktop replay's own exports to
simulate a refusal and recomputes `jitterPx` through the frozen metrics module.
Nothing in the tracker or the backend was touched, so this is the probe, not the
treatment. The maintainer's rulings of the same day on its results are folded
in below, each marked. The brief asked for the probe as a message only; the
protocol requires this file, and the maintainer ruled the brief wrong on that.

The computation is
[`2026-10-16-104-stage1-probe.mjs`](./2026-10-16-104-stage1-probe.mjs), which
reads the exports and the frozen module and writes nothing. The exports are
desktop replays and are not committed (`AGENTS.md`, "Test assets").

## The setup, and the checks that make the rest readable

- **Build:** a clean detached worktree at `37dc6d4`, round 0's commit, `npm ci`
  and `npm run build` on node v24.21.0. Its manifest read HEAD `37dc6d4`,
  clean, bundle `3a2567c15d59…`: byte for byte what #123's seed test built.
- **Runs:** `scripts/replay-clips.mjs --external 70 --seed <n> --packages
  bundle --out <dir>` for seeds 1 to 8, #123's procedure. Read: the "every
  frame, sync" arm on each of the three bundled clips, over counted loops 1 to
  4. Every count below is from a seeded run, and the seed is stated with it.
- **The metric is imported, not reimplemented.** `cornerJitter`,
  `countedLoopFrames` and `unwrapMediaTimes` are imported unchanged from
  `examples/js/bench-metrics.mjs`, which is identical at `37dc6d4` and at this
  commit. Each filter is `cornerJitter`'s own `include` argument.
- **Reproduction check:** all 24 recorded `runSummary.jitterPx` values (three
  clips, eight seeds) reproduce to the last bit when the counted frames are
  passed through the imported `cornerJitter`, and the eight values per clip are
  #123's table. The check is `base.jitterPx === e.runSummary.jitterPx`; it can
  fail, and it did not.
- **Window-keying check:** windows kept and frames per window are read by
  keying the kept frames as `cornerJitter` does and applying its two
  eligibility rules. The count so obtained equals the module's `jitterWindows`
  on every filter of every seed of every clip (the script asserts it per
  filter). It can fail, and it did not.
- **The signal:** `H` per posed frame is recovered from the export's four
  corners, which determine it; the recovered `H` reprojects the corners to at
  most 2.3 × 10⁻⁸ px (table clip) and 1.4 × 10⁻¹² px (wall clip). `K` is the
  bundle's `intrinsics(width, height)` at the export's processing resolution
  (480 × 270 for the wall clip, 203 × 360 for the others). `d_scale` and
  `d_orth` are #104's, on `B = K⁻¹H`.

**The filters.**

- **A ≥ N** refuses a DETECT frame with fewer than N inliers and nothing else.
  It is the brief's filter.
- **B ≥ N** refuses every frame whose lock was born from such a detection: a
  TRACK frame inherits the inlier count of the most recent DETECT frame before
  it. *Beyond the brief.* It is the pessimistic reading of a refusal.
- **S, O, E at τ** refuse a DETECT frame whose |`d_scale`|, |`d_orth`|, or
  either, exceeds τ. *Beyond the brief.* They are #104's own gate, as the probe
  can read it.

Dispersion over the eight seeds is the SD on 7 degrees of freedom with its 95%
interval (χ² quantiles 1.690 and 16.013). *Beyond the brief:* the brief asked
for the values and the ratio.

## 1. Responsiveness

**From the code** (`examples/js/bench-metrics.mjs`):

- Lines 933 to 934: `read = (f) => f.corners && include(f)`;
  `posed = frames.filter(read)`. A refused frame leaves the posed set.
- Line 954: each kept frame joins the window
  `` `${loop}:${Math.floor(f.mediaTimeSeconds / JITTER_WINDOW_S)}` ``.
- Line 963: a window with `n < 4` is skipped. Line 969: a window whose media
  times have zero spread is skipped.
- Lines 985 and 991: `dof += n − 2` per kept window;
  `jitterPx = √(residuals ÷ (4 · dof))`, the residuals being each kept
  window's least-squares line fit per corner.
- Line 165 (`frameRecord`):
  `corners: result.ok ? reprojectCorners(result.H, targetPoints) : null`;
  line 88: `reprojectCorners` returns `null` when the four corners do not fall
  on one side of the camera.

So a refusal reaches `jitterPx` through two channels, not one: the frame leaves
the residual pool and the line fit of a window that stays eligible, and a window
left with fewer than four frames leaves the pool. Never through `H`'s values.
The brief said "through window eligibility" alone; on the wall clip that
channel carries nothing (below).

**Empirically.** Filter A ≥ 5, the brief's "plausible N":

## 5. `ok` and "has an outline" diverge by four in five (a finding of its own, by the maintainer's ruling)

Over the eight seeds' counted loops, DETECT frames with `ok: true` whose
`corners` are `null`, because `reprojectCorners` found the outline on both
sides of the camera (line 88): **1,827 of 2,276 on the wall clip and 1,304 of
1,562 on the table clip.** Roughly four in five of the gate's accepted
detections never reach `jitterPx`, yet they count as ok frames in
`trackTimeShare` and `detectionLocks`. That is not blindness: those metrics
measure something else. But "ok" and "has a usable outline" diverge by 80%,
and #104's coverage claim, registered as "posed frames over the clip's frames
on the same media times" (the plan, "#104: a joint claim, or none"), has to
say which of the two it is counting. The question is stated here; no answer is
proposed.

## 6. The gap between refusing a detection and refusing its lock: a stated limit for #104's registration (by the maintainer's ruling)

Filter A refuses a detection; filter B refuses the lock it started. The real
treatment sits between the two, because the tracker re-detects on the next
frame, and the exports cannot show where. *The B figures and the lock-origin
attribution are beyond the brief.*

**Filter B.** Wall clip: 36 of 36 windows at B ≥ 8 (its locks are all born
from detections of 8 or more inliers), 20 to 24 at B ≥ 10. Table clip:
**14, 7, 0, 7, 0, 0, 0, 7 of 33 at B ≥ 8, and 0 on seven of eight seeds at
B ≥ 10**; the clip has no `jitterPx` at all there.

**The detections that start the locks.** Attributing each TRACK run that
touches the counted loops to the DETECT frame before it:

- wall: 64 lasting locks (50 or more counted frames; eight per seed), all born
  from detections of 8 or more inliers (8: 23, 9: 8, 11: 1, 66: 32), all with
  corners; |d_scale| 0.003 to 0.450, |d_orth| 0.000 to 0.233. Both |d| ≤ 0.05
  on 32 of them, ≤ 0.1 on 52, ≤ 0.2 on 58.
- table: 32 lasting locks (four per seed, 202 to 221 frames each), born from
  6-inlier detections on 20, 7-inlier on 7, 9-inlier on 4, 13-inlier on 1, all
  with corners; |d_scale| 0.030 to 0.114, |d_orth| 0.013 to 0.058. Both
  |d| ≤ 0.05 on 5, ≤ 0.1 on 25, **≤ 0.2 on all 32.**
- On both clips the 4- and 5-inlier detections start locks of one frame (12
  such runs on the table clip, none lasting).

So the gate that cleans the metric best, τ = 0.05, would refuse 27 of the table
clip's 32 lock births and 32 of the wall clip's 64. **The rulings:**

- **The coverage bound is stated at a threshold that preserves lock births,**
  not at the one that cleans best. On these figures that is τ = 0.2: ratio 1.28,
  SD 0.221 [0.146, 0.450] px on the table clip, ratio 1.01, SD 0.012
  [0.008, 0.025] px on the wall clip, every lasting lock's birth on the table
  clip and 58 of 64 on the wall clip passing.
- **The registration says plainly that the treatment's behaviour after a
  refusal is unmeasured and lies between A and B,** and names what would
  measure it: the first implementation reports, per refused detection, whether
  the next frame re-detects and whether the lock survives. That is the one
  piece of #104 that cannot be probed before implementation.

## 7. The jitter clause does not transfer to the clips round 1 will use (the probe's most important result, by the maintainer's ruling)

The null control's jitter clause (the plan,
["The null control's jitter clause (2026-10-14)"](./README.md#the-null-controls-jitter-clause-2026-10-14))
was calibrated on the static clip: round 0's s₀ = 0.0012672 px, its operational
arm threshold A = 0.0019834 px, its k = 2 threshold 0.0042375 px. The static
clip cannot express #104 (item 4). So round 1 of #104 must run on the hand-held
clips, whose residual after the refusal, 5.30 to 5.33 px on the wall clip and
2.33 to 2.36 px on the table clip (A ≥ 12 and A ≥ 8, seeds 1 to 8), is 42 and
19 times the static clip's 0.124939 px, and 2,700 and 1,200 times the
operational threshold A. **The provisional threshold does not transfer to the
clips round 1 will use, and round 1 needs its own calibration on the clips it
uses.** No new threshold is derived here: that is a separate measurement with
its own degrees of freedom. The same is noted in the plan beside the clause.
