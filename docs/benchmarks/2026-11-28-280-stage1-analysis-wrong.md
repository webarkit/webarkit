## Condition 3 on the synthetic sequences and the mirrored frames

Per set and lever, on the lever's own states: eligible states (the lever can respond; Fs cannot respond here, a = b on every state), the shipped step's and the lever's confirmed and wrong outputs. Wrong on the sequences: a confirmed output more than RIGHT = 2 px RMS off the truth (`inView`); on mirrored frames every confirmation. The "no more wrong" half is void on a set where the lever has fewer than 10 eligible states, and on a set with no state at the lever's age.
