  | **V**, velocity | `previous` = the `H` of the detection on the processed frame before the lock's own, when there is one (a DETECT chain); else as shipped | no: ruling 4a |
  | **R1**, reach by the cutoff | `tukeyC` 8 for that step | no (proposed, not ruled) |
  | **R2**, reach by the fit's start | the same observations; the fit started off the prediction: an IRLS at `tukeyC` 12 from the prediction, then the shipped fit (`tukeyC` 4) from its `H`, judged by the shipped rules | no (proposed, not ruled) |

  | **S**, the starting `H` | the detection's `H` refitted on its own inliers (`robustHomography`, Tukey 4) | no (proposed, as 4a) |
  | **T**, retry | when a refused first step's frame fails to detect, the refused detection's `H` kept for one more first step on the next processed frame | yes: ruling 4b, a pending lock; #143's rename travels with it |

  the shipped arm must be a frame on which that arm ran a first step, fatally

  through the same wrapping with no lever must equal `replayRun`'s frame for frame, fatally.
- **Charged costs:** on the device schedules, a frame's extra alignment (R3's wide pass) is charged
  one more `stepMs`, and T's retry step is a step (`result.tracking`), so neither lever wins by
  skipping fewer frames (M3's round 1 trap).

### What each lever can move, from the code, before any run

- **V** changes the prediction only where a DETECT chain exists: the prediction is
  `predictHomography(previous, current)`, frame-indexed (one processed frame of motion,
  `predict_homography.ts`). Elsewhere it is the shipped step.
- **R1 and R2** act on the fit only. `too-few-patches` has two exits in `trackFrame`
  (`track_frame.ts` 401, before the fit: fewer than 8 observations; 421, after it: fewer than 8
  inliers). **A fit-side lever cannot touch the first:** the observations come from the alignment,
  which starts at the same prediction. The refusals are split by `rmsError === null`.
- **R3** re-aligns from the wide fit's `H`, so it can touch both exits.
- **S** changes the start of the alignment and the fit. The detection's `H` is already a linear
  refit on its inliers (`find_homography`, `packages/cv-backend-jsfeatnext/src/jsfeatnext_backend.ts`,
  the comment above its restarts), so S replaces an algebraic least-squares fit with a geometric
  robust one: a small change is expected. The synthetic grids say whether the refit is closer to
  the truth.
- **T** can act only where a first step is refused and its frame's detection then fails. Its
  eligible count is printed first; where the clips re-detect on the refused frame (wall's DETECT
  chains), it does nothing.

### The definitions

- **Lock rate:** `summarizeRun(frames, { clipDurationS }).trackShare` over the run's two loops,
  `clipDurationS` the clip's loop period.
- **Re-lock latency** (`relockLatency`, defined in the analysis script and self-tested both ways):
  a **loss** is a processed frame that is not `TRACK` after one that is, other than the first frame
  after a loop wrap (`wrapsOf`); its latency is the processed frames from it to the next `TRACK`
  frame, and the media time between them on the unwrapped timeline. A loss never followed by
  `TRACK` in the run is **censored**: counted, with no latency. Per run: the median, frames and ms.
  Between arms on the every-frame schedule it is also read **on the shipped arm's loss events**
  (`latencyOnEvents`), because a lever that adds short locks adds losses with short latencies of
  its own.
- **Share of first steps confirmed:** `lockSteps(frames).firstSteps`, over the run (a diagnostic,
  ruling 2) and on **paired detections**: the ladder's rows, the same detection handed to the
  shipped step and to the lever's step on the same frame.
- **Right and wrong on synthetic frames:** `inView(H, T)` against the truth; a detection is right
  within 2 px RMS (ruling 2). The confirmed step's output `H` is scored too: a confirmed step whose
  output is more than 2 px off is a wrong acceptance whatever its detection.

## The stop conditions, written before any counted run

By Stage 1's rule the step stops when the metric cannot respond or the corpus cannot express the
effect. Here, before any figure:

1. **The metric's spread is larger than every lever's effect.** On each **deciding cell** (table
   every frame; table's device schedules, read once if its device-15 and device-25 runs give the
   same frames, as #262 found, else each; wall every frame, device-15 and device-25), each lever's
   lock rate is compared **per seed**: d = lever − shipped on the same seed. A lever **moves** a cell
   when the 95% interval of d̄ (7 degrees of freedom) excludes 0 **and** |d̄| exceeds the shipped
   arm's own s over seeds on that cell. On **paired detections** (per clip and schedule, seeds
   summed), a lever moves the share confirmed when its discordant counts (lever only against
   shipped only) differ with a two-sided exact sign test p < 0.05. **The step stops on the metric**
   if no lever moves any deciding cell upward and no lever moves paired detections upward. The
   count of seeds outside the shipped range is printed, as descriptive only.
2. **No lever confirms more right detections on synthetic frames without confirming wrong ones.**
   Per lever, on the synthetic sets of question 4: it confirms more first steps from right
   detections than the shipped step, **and** confirms no more from wrong ones, **and** no more of
   its confirmed outputs are over 2 px, **and** it accepts no wrong fit in the perturbation sweep
   (the shipped configuration accepts none of 1,370), **and** it accepts no more on mirrored frames
   than the shipped step (tracking runs and the stress set). A set with fewer than **10** wrong
   detections cannot show "no wrong one confirmed": that half is **void, not passed**, on it. **The
   step stops on correctness** if no lever meets the first part with every applicable second part
   met or void, and the void sets are named.
3. **The corpus cannot separate lag from the starting `H`.** On a clip and schedule, per seed,
   d_lag = (share of first steps confirmed at +0) − (share confirmed at the run's own lag). The
   corpus separates the two on that clip **if both hold:** the 95% interval of d̄_lag excludes 0
   (lag costs confirmations), **and** at least one first step is refused at +0 on every seed (the
   starting `H` costs confirmations with no lag). If neither clip separates them on any schedule,
   that question stops here, and no lever is chosen for one cause over the other.

Added, also before any figure:

4. **A lever that admits more wrong locks drops:** a lever whose TRACK frames on mirrored tracking
   runs, or whose confirmations in the mirrored stress set, exceed the shipped step's on any clip
   (seeds summed) is not proposed for Stage 2–3, whatever condition 1 says.
5. **A lever whose prototype touches held steps is not read:** condition met by the fatal
   divergence check above; a run that fails it produces no file.

The verdict follows from these: the step goes on with the lever that moves the metric (1) without
failing (2) or (4), and says whether it opens the state machine; or it stops, and says what that
leaves.
