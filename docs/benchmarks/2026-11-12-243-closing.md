## 3. What the outcomes mean, and their limits

In the registration's words. Nothing below is stronger than its source.

- **A direction, not a magnitude.** "So no criterion registers a magnitude. Each registers a direction
  against the device's own spread. The replay figure stands beside the device's d̄ as a reading."
- **P1, CF − IPPE below 10°.** "A held P1 says IPPE's pose moves more about its own line below 10° on
  this device, with this `K`. It does not say IPPE's pose is further from the truth." The registration
  says this of P1. P2 and P3 are the same comparison, on the table and cross clips, and it gives them
  no sentence of their own.
- **P4 and P5, the guard across the crossing.** "Stage 1 conjectures, without measuring it, why the
  guard reads above both arms. The 4.41 px step falls inside one one-second window, [8, 9) s, where the
  metric fits one line through both estimators. These two criteria predict that reading on the device.
  They do not claim that the guard is less stable at rest."
- **P6.** Held is the registration's count: one switch per counted loop, inside the window. Its
  refuting outcome, a loop with three or more switches in at least two of the 8 runs ("Three or more
  is the guard switching back and forth near 10°."), was met in no run.
- **P7.** "A held P7 says the filtered pose moves less. It says nothing of the delay the filter adds."
- **The wall clip.** Descriptive, by the ruling of 2026-11-10: "A prediction on a sign the corpus
  already shows unstable would be read as a criterion that "failed", where the record already knows
  the corpus cannot express it".

**The limits,** as the registration and the records state them:

- **Stability only, no accuracy and no lag.** "Every criterion is in jitter, and `poseJitterPx` reads
  jitter, not accuracy, and cannot see a constant offset, nor at a constant rate a constant lag
  (`DEFINITIONS.poseJitterPx`)."
- **`K` is assumed.** "Every figure of this campaign uses `intrinsics(w, h)`, 60° of horizontal field
  of view". "The selection "below 10°" and the switch's place on cross therefore hold for this `K`
  only. No result here says where IPPE or CF is better for a real camera. That is #109's."
- **One seed.** Every counted run is `?seed=1`: "the device figures belong to seed 1's draws at the
  media times the device detected."
- **Generality over seeds rests on Stage 1.** "Generality over seeds rests on Stage 1's replays, seeds
  1 to 8, where every readable comparison kept one sign over all eight. The device runs measure the
  device's run-to-run spread at one seed."
- **One block per clip, in one session.** "a held result covers one block in one session. It cannot
  say whether the direction holds in another state of the device".
- **A conjecture, not measured,** under every paired difference: that "a state that moves between
  runs, such as heat or the slow state #123 holds, does not enter a paired difference". "#123's state
  is unexplained, so whether it could act on IPPE and CF differently on the same frame is not known."
- **Frame fidelity's share sits beside every spread:** 25 of 1,145 pairs, 2.18%, a lower bound (D3,
  above). By the registration it "reaches no criterion. It reaches only the spread between runs".
- **The tilt clips' acquisition was measured in the pre-flight:** the cross run's `acquire` p50 over
  counted loops is 28.2 ms, against the borrowed 36.9 ms (the pre-flight's item 2). The registration,
  on what changes if it differs: "only the label. P3 to P6's basis is a replay on an acquisition the
  device does not have, and the record says by how much." In the counted cross runs the same reading
  is 27.8 to 28.7 ms (session A's V9 table).
- **The thermal reading** (V9, a reading, not a gate): no block is flagged against M3's bounds, quoted
  as M3's (the two session records' V9 tables).
