# #239's probe: does the backend hand `targetSide` an `H` that is not finite, or whose determinant overflows, underflows or loses its sign? (2026-11-08)

**A Stage 1 probe under [the step protocol](./step-protocol.md), run on 2026-11-08: a desktop replay,
headless, RANSAC seeded per frame, seeds 1 to 8, and the ground truth's sequences. Not campaign data;
never reportable as a round. It measures and registers nothing: no criterion, no prediction, no change
under `packages/*/src`. What `detectTarget` should do about #239 is the maintainer's decision after
these figures (M9's milestone description, "Before the first counted run", item 2).**

The computation is [`2026-11-08-239-probe.mjs`](./2026-11-08-239-probe.mjs). Every figure below is in
its stdout unless it is labelled as a post-run read. Two runs at `6b32107` gave byte-identical stdout,
121 lines, SHA-256 **`baff28399c650b20105867ffd3975b3ab8c54a98c0e1bdb62409e5085677391c`**. The per-`H`
rows (28,617, about 17 MB) went to the run directory and are not committed.

## The figures first

- **Zero, in every class, on every input.** Of **28,617** `H` the backend returned to detection, none has
  a non-finite entry or a non-finite `w` on the grid `targetSide` reads. None has a non-finite computed
  determinant, or a computed determinant whose sign differs from the scaled one's or from the exact
  one's. None has a computed determinant that underflows to 0. **22,366** of them reached `targetSide`,
  and **18,709** were accepted.
- **How far the corpus is from the edges.** On the `H` that reach `targetSide`, `|det H|` runs from
  1.117e-11 to 2.022e7 and the largest entry from 10.25 to 6.536e4. A double overflows near 1.8e308 and
  underflows to subnormals below 2.2e-308. The smallest computed determinant that clears 0 does so by
  1.117e-11, and the same `H`'s scaled determinant, `det (H / max |Hᵢ|)`, by 5.415e-19, the smallest
  scaled one too.
- **The 575 determinants that are exactly 0** are the 575 fits RANSAC refused (`ok: false`). Each of them
  has an all-zero `H` (post-run read), and none reaches `targetSide`.
- **What `targetSide` returned** on the 22,366 it read: 18,709 `"facing"`, 191 `"reversed"`, 3,466
  `"mixed-w"`, 0 `"singular"`.
- **The stateless mode is not measured:** `scripts/replay-clips.mjs` cannot drive it.

## The method

1. **What is captured.** `estimateHomography` is wrapped on the injected backend's prototype, between
   the contract and the backend's own code. The backend is untouched, as ADR-0001 injects it. The wrapper
   copies `H`, `ok` and `numInliers` and returns the backend's object unchanged.
   - In `packages/nft-tracker/src`, `detectTarget` is the only caller (`detection.ts:514`), so every call
     is a detection's, refused or not.
   - `NftTracker.prototype.process` is wrapped to read the frame and the tracker's own `setup.grid`, the
     points `targetSide` reads.
   - `NftTracker.prototype.detected` is wrapped to read what `detectTarget` returned.
   - Every wrapper passes all its arguments through.
   - **Completeness:** every frame whose own detection got past `too-few-matches` must have exactly
     one `H` captured, and no other frame one (fatal, every seed, and run by run on the ground truth).
     A call that escaped the wrapper, as a backend's own-property method would, fails it.
2. **The replay.** The frame loop is `scripts/replay-run.mjs`'s `replayRun`, and the seeding is
   `scripts/replay-seeding.mjs`'s `runSeeding` and `countGlobalRandomReads`, imported.
   - The decoding (`probe`, `greyFrames`, `loadClip`) and the schedules' constants live in
     `scripts/replay-clips.mjs`, a top-level script that cannot be imported. They are copied, with their
     lines at `6bccf51` cited in the script, and check D proves them frame by frame.
   - **Default form:** the three bundled clips, two loops, tracking on every frame and on the 15 and
     25 ms steps, and detection-only on every frame and on the device schedule.
   - **`--clips tilt`:** the three tilt clips, six loops on the loops' continuous timeline, every frame
     and the two fixed steps, at the acquisition constant borrowed from the static clip.
   - Seeds 1 to 8, `--seed-policy frame`, the tracker at its defaults, `K = intrinsics(w, h)`.
3. **The ground truth.** The sequences the references script records, in its order, are copied from
   `docs/benchmarks/2026-11-06-228-133-stage3-references.mjs`, through its loader. The copy is proved by
   R-truth, with recording off and on. The sweep (`shippedSweep`) made 0 detection calls; a call there
   would be fatal, since it has no tracker's grid to classify against.
4. **Every replay run is done twice in one process,** with the wrappers installed both times, recording
   off and then on. The frame records must be equal, with the clock's fields (`timings`,
   `trackerTimings`) masked. This shows that recording changes nothing. That the wrappers' presence
   changes nothing is shown against outputs made without them: check D, against the replay's own
   exports at seed 1, and R-truth, against the references script.
5. **Each `H` is classified** by `classify`:
   1. a non-finite entry;
   2. a non-finite `w` on any of the 25 grid points (2a: at one of the four corners);
   3. a non-finite computed determinant with finite entries;
   4. the sign of the determinant as `targetSide` computes it (its expression and order of operations),
      against that of `det (H / max |Hᵢ|)` (4a: against the exact sign);
   5. a computed determinant of exactly 0 whose scaled one is not.

   Classes 3 and 4, and 4 and 5, can hold together: an overflow to the wrong infinity is both, and an
   underflow to 0 against a nonzero scaled determinant is a sign lost.
6. **The outcome.** Each `H`'s outcome under `detectTarget`'s rule (`ok`, at least
   `MIN_DETECTION_INLIERS`, `"facing"`) must equal what the tracker's `detected()` received (fatal). An
   `H` "reaches `targetSide`" when it passes the first two.

## What this probe does not establish

- **Other devices.** Nothing ran on one. The fixed-step schedules model a device's frame selection, not
  its arithmetic. The `H` are the desktop's, on Node v24.21.0's V8. The determinant is ordinary
  double-precision arithmetic, but no device's result is read here.
- **Other backends.** Only `cv-backend-jsfeatnext` on jsfeatNext 1.2.0. Another backend's
  `estimateHomography`, under the same contract, may return what this one does not.
- **A real moving camera beyond the corpus.** Six bundled clips and the ground truth's synthetic paths.
  The tilt clips' device schedules use a borrowed acquisition constant.
- **Whether a case could occur on frames the corpus does not contain.** Zero on 28,617 `H` bounds
  nothing about inputs outside them. The margins say how far these `H` are from the edges, not that no
  input comes nearer.
- **Constructed inputs to `estimateHomography` itself,** which #239's body asks for beside the corpus
  ("on the corpus and on constructed inputs"). This probe gave constructed `H` to the classes, not
  constructed correspondences to the backend. That half of the issue's question is open.
- **The stateless mode, the scaled-step schedule, `--external`'s and `--transfer`'s arms, and
  `--seed-policy run`.** None ran.
- **Unseeded runs.** Every count is from a seeded run, as Stage 1 requires.
- **What `detectTarget` should do.** No fix is proposed.
