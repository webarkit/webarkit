## The questions

1. Does setting a lock's starting velocity better keep locks on fast footage, read on paired events
   several steps deep: V at the first step after a detection, F (F0, Fs, Ffb) at the first held step?
2. Does a lever that keeps more locks also admit more wrong ones?
3. What the descriptive lock rate per seed shows beside it, and what M9's recorded device states
   show, as information.
4. The verdict: V, one form of F, or neither going on to a registration, with whether it opens the
   state machine; or the step stopped, and what that leaves; then TEBLID's and V's reopening
   conditions read on the result.

## The definitions

- **The unit: a paired event,** as #278 defines it: a distinct lock state for a lever, within a clip
  and schedule (its key: the lever, the clip frame, `previous` and `current` bit for bit, V's chain
  `H`, Fs's `a` and `b`). Repeats (the second loop, seeds reaching the same state) are marked and not
  counted. **The lock rate per seed** is descriptive only: printed with its spread, deciding nothing.
- **Eligible:** an event the lever can respond on, from the code (`canRespond` in this probe's
  analysis): V where `eligible` (a chain); F0 and Ffb every age-1 state; Fs where `a ≠ b` and its
  power was taken. Printed per lever and cell **before any class.**
- **Steps across a loop wrap are counted apart and excluded from every deciding figure.** The clip
  jumps back to its start between them, so they are an artefact of the replay's loop, not motion. The
  exclusion is read on **the lever's whole input span,** from the record two before the step (the
  lock's detection at age 1, V's chain detection at age 0) to the step itself (`spanWrap`), not only
  on #278's `acrossWrap` hop from the record before: a detection in the previous loop is a wrap
  artefact with `acrossWrap` false. Their count is reported per lever and cell, eligible and
  discordant. Carries that reach a loop's end are censored by #278's `deep` (below). In `wrong` (the
  synthetic and mirrored sets) a wrong output is wrong wherever it falls, and nothing is excluded.
- **Whether kept locks last:** `deep`, N = `LASTING` = 50 (#273's analysis, line 87 at `a773629`, 88 since `fadd4a4`), through #278's
  `stepsToLast` (a carry from a paired step at age `a` runs at most `N − (a + 1)` steps). For each
  counted discordant event, the arm that kept is carried on with the shipped step:
  - **a wrong lock** if any of its kept steps (the paired step included) is beyond the gross margin on
    a frame the reference accepts, whatever the carry's outcome;
  - else **a win for that arm** if it lasts N;
  - else **a tie** if it is lost before N;
  - else **censored** at a loop's end: excluded from the primary figure and counted by reason.
  - **Concordant events are ties** (both arms kept, or both refused) and are not carried.
- **"Wrong" on the step's output `H`:**
  - on #273's synthetic sequences: a confirmed output more than `RIGHT` = 2 px RMS off the truth by
    `inView` (#273's definition, `2026-11-14-272-stage1-analysis.mjs` line 481 at `a773629`, 482 since `fadd4a4`; no new threshold);
  - on #273's mirrored frames, every confirmation is wrong. **The mirrored runs include the fast
    clip;**
  - on the clips: gross errors only, against the segmented reference, by the margin below.
- **M9's recorded states:** #278's `m9`, information only, on reproduced states only. Their rows
  enter no stop condition and no verdict.

## The two parameters

### `--gross-margin`: **10 processing px**, on the mean corner distance

Derived from these sources and no other: #284's record (its wall and table statements, and why
3.75 px over-reads), `artoolkit-nft-bench`'s noise floor
(`data/videos/tab9-2026-11-26/README.md` at `46bd6d6`, "`segment.py`'s noise floor on this session's
footage"), and the fast record's bottom-left bias. Processing px are 0.375 of 1280×720 px on the wall
and fast clips (480×270).

1. **The reference's own error, corner by corner, on the fast clip** (the clip with the largest
   stated error): the paper's offset from the print (#284: "three corners 1 to 2 px inward", taken as
   2 px at 1280×720, 0.75 processing px), plus the larger of the stated segmentation error ("about 1-2
   px on sharp frames", 2 px, 0.75 processing px) and the noise floor's p99 (TL 1.28, TR 1.20, BR
   8.16, BL 2.28 px at 1280×720: 0.48, 0.45, 3.06, 0.855 processing px); on BL, the fast record's bias
   of up to 11 processing px in place of the offset:
   - TL 0.75 + 0.75 = 1.50; TR 0.75 + 0.75 = 1.50; BR 0.75 + 3.06 = 3.81; BL 11 + 0.855 = 11.86;
   - **mean of four: 18.67 / 4 = 4.67 px;** mean of TL, TR, BR: 6.81 / 3 = 2.27 px.
2. **#284's over-read band:** on the wall, the records beyond 3.75 px other than the two far ones sit
   at 3.81 to 7.41 px, "and the bottom-left corner carries most of the distance". The wall's BL offset
   is 8 px at 1280×720, 3 processing px on one corner, 0.75 on the mean. The fast clip's BL bias is up
   to 11 on one corner, 2.75 on the mean: the band's top moves by 2.75 − 0.75 = 2.00, **to 9.41 px.**
3. **The margin is 10 px:** 9.41 rounded up to the next whole processing pixel.
   - **Constraint 1, the bottom-left bias alone, with the noise floor, cannot carry a row across
     it:** 4.67 < 10 and 9.41 < 10 (by 0.59 px, above the last significant digit).
   - **Constraint 2, a lock off the poster must cross it:** a quad shifted along x by **a quarter of
     its own TL–TR width** has a mean corner distance of a quarter of the width; the smallest accepted
     poster on any clip is 48.8 px wide (wall; table 70.0, fast 154.2), so a quarter is at least
     12.2 px > 10.
4. **Exercised both ways on the reference files, with no tracker** (`2026-11-28-280-stage1-analysis.mjs
   margin`; its output at this commit is below): on every accepted row of the wall (291), table (267)
   and fast (879) references, the quad with its BL corner moved by 11 processing px reads 2.75 px and
   does not cross; the quad shifted by a quarter of its width crosses on every row. The check itself
   fails when it must: with the margin set to 2 px the BL move crosses on all three clips, and at
   13 px the quarter-width shift fails on the wall (both runs on a scratch copy of the script).
5. **Against #284's recorded gross rows** (wall frame 191, 34.43 px, 0.234 of its poster's width;
   wall 271, 31.48 px, 0.169; table 56, 13.80 px, 0.171; table 55, a detection, 18.48 px, 0.230): a
   shift by the smallest of these fractions, 0.169, crosses on every fast and table row and on the
   wall from frame 51 on; on the wall's frames 0–50, where the poster is narrowest, it does not. **A
   limit of the control clip's earliest frames: a gross error of under 10 px there is not flagged.**
6. **The second reading, a sensitivity check:** the mean of TL, TR and BR only, on every clip,
   against the same 10 px (`cornerDistances`; BL's bias is outside it, and its worst case above is
   2.27 px). If the two readings disagree on a deciding figure, the verdict says so.
7. **What the derivation does not cover:** blur, whose cost to the segmentation no source states (the
   fast clip is the blurriest of the three; its reference is segmented on the source, not the CRF 28
   bench file); the noise floor is frame-to-frame jitter on a sharp static clip, not accuracy; and the
   table has no stated error of its own (#284), so the fast clip's figures are carried over to it.

### `--repro-threshold`: **2 processing px**, by principle

A recorded state is **reproduced** when the desktop's output corners land on the device's as closely
as #273 asks a right output to land on the truth: `RIGHT` = 2 px, the existing distance at which
this series calls a step's output right (`2026-11-14-272-stage1-analysis.mjs` line 481 at `a773629`). Fixed from
that principle, without data: **no M9 distance, shipped or lever, was read** before this commit, and
no `m9` output exists yet. It decides only which M9 states inform, and M9's rows enter no condition.

## The stop conditions, written before any counted run

1. **The corpus cannot express the effect.**
   - A lever's cell is **void, not refuted,** when it has fewer than **6 counted discordant events**
     (eligible, not across a wrap, not repeats): below 6 a two-sided exact sign test cannot reach
     p < 0.05.
   - **Added before any run:** a cell with 6 or more events but **fewer than 6 wins in all** (lever
     and shipped) cannot reach p < 0.05 either, and is **void (wins)**, not "does not move"
     (step-protocol.md line 294). The label is new here and flagged for the maintainer.
   - **The step stops on the corpus** if every lever is void on every fast-clip cell. The verdict then
     says what corpus would express it.
   - Each lever's eligible events per cell are printed first.
2. **The lever does not better keep locks.** The deciding cells are **the fast clip's `every`,
   `device-15` and `device-25`.**
   - A lever **moves** a cell when its wins exceed the shipped step's wins on that cell's counted
     discordant events, with a two-sided exact sign test p < 0.05 (wins against wins; ties, wrong
     locks and censored carries excluded and counted).
   - **For Ffb the shipped wins are 0 by construction** (its discordance is one-sided): its p says
     only that 6 or more of its rescued locks lasted N, not that it beat a symmetric alternative. A
     failed fallback's extra `stepMs` appears in no paired event, only in the descriptive lock rate.
   - **The step stops on the metric** if no lever moves any deciding cell.
   - Every figure is stated with its counts and margin (step-protocol.md, "A figure is stated with its
     margin").
   - **The independent attempt at refuting each deciding figure, registered now:** (a) the same test
     on wins **collapsed to distinct clip frames** (a frame counts once for an arm if any of its events
     is that arm's win), because the eight seeds on one clip frame are not independent; (b) an
     independent recount of the cell from the `paired` and `deep` rows by a fresh agent not given this
     analysis script. Beside each deciding figure: the three-corner reading and the reading that counts
     censored carries as wins (a sensitivity check, not deciding). **When any of them disagrees with
     the main reading, the verdict says so.**
3. **A lever admits more wrong locks.** A lever is **not proposed**, whatever condition 2 says, if on
   any set it gives more wrong outputs than the shipped step:
   - on the synthetic sequences: confirmed outputs more than 2 px off, on the lever's own states;
   - on the mirrored frames (seeds summed), per clip, fast included;
   - on the clips: more wrong locks among its carries than among the shipped step's carries, on the
     same cell's counted discordant events.
   - **Void, not passed:** a set where the shipped step can produce fewer than 10 wrong outputs cannot
     show "no more wrong". On the synthetic and mirrored sets, that is fewer than 10 states the lever
     can respond on (the shipped and lever steps run from the same states); on a clip cell, fewer than
     10 carries of the shipped arm. The void sets are named. A lever with more wrong outputs on a void
     set is still not proposed.
4. **The existing clips as a control.** The table and the wall are expected to show nothing (the
   milestone: "The existing clips are too slow to show a fix"). A lever whose shipped wins exceed its
   own wins there, with a two-sided exact sign test p < 0.05, is **not proposed**, and the verdict
   names the cell.
5. **The fast clip's device cells are modelled.** Their acquisition is borrowed from the wall
   (`FAST_BORROWED_ACQUIRE_MS`), not measured, and every place they are read says "modelled". If a
   lever moves only device cells of the fast clip, it may go on, but **its registration starts with a
   measured acquisition of the fast clip on the Tab 9**, and the verdict says so.
6. **A prototype that touches steps outside its age is not read:** the fatal checks enforce it
   (#278's, which reproduce the shipped step on every state and check each state against the
   records; the F runs' `divergenceRefusalF`). A run that fails one produces no file.

**The verdict follows from these:** V, one form of F, or neither goes on to a registration, with
whether it opens the state machine (neither does, above); or the step stops, and says what that
leaves. Then **TEBLID's and V's reopening conditions are read on the result:** TEBLID's, "deferred
until the first step after a detection holds better" (ADR-0001 line 235), and V's, "deferred until a
corpus with fast motion and ground truth" (line 252). **The fast clip has fast motion and no ground
truth** (its reference informs, it does not judge pixels): the ground-truth half of V's condition is
unmet by this corpus, whatever the figures say.
