# #278, M12's Stage 1 tooling (2026-11-22)

**Instruments for #280, built and tested on the existing clips. No verdict, no criterion and no
lever figure:** every mode writes rows, and none sums them into a reading for or against a lever.
The trial that showed the tools run end to end is not committed; its end-to-end counts are in the
PR, labelled a trial. Nothing under `packages/` or `examples/` changes.

The script: [`2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs`](./2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs); its mode-level
negatives: [`2026-11-22-278-stage1-negatives.mjs`](./2026-11-22-278-stage1-negatives.mjs). The

(`readConstant`: exactly one `const NAME = <literal>;`, or it refuses): the runner's `LOOPS`,

## The definitions, as built

- **A lock's age:** 0 at the first step after a detection (`previous` is `null`), 1 at the first
  held step, and so on: from the runner's `steps` (`first`, and each held step following a kept
  one on the record before, fatally), and from the records' states on M9's exports and the
  synthetic sequences (the `TRACK` records since the lock's DETECT record).
- **A lock state:** the step's `previous`, `current` and frame.
- **V, at age 0 only:** #273's V, `firstStep("V", …)`: `previous` the `H` of the DETECT record on
  the processed frame before the lock's own detection (a chain, as #273's ladder reads it). On
  any other first step V is the shipped step, and its row says `eligible: false`.
- **F0, at age 1 only:** `trackFrame(frame, track, current, current)`; the prediction is `current`.
- **Fs, at age 1 only:** the velocity scaled to the interval it is applied over. The motion from
  `previous` to `current` is `M = current · previous⁻¹`, measured over `a` clip frames (the
  detection's frame to the first step's) and applied over `b` (the first step's frame to this
  one); the prediction is `M^(b/a) · current` and the step `trackFrame(frame, track, null, it)`.
  - `a` and `b` are clip frames on the loops' timeline, each processed frame placed by its media
    time. **This reads the brief's "on the clip's media times" as positions, counted in frames,**
    which is how the milestone states the mismatch ("measured over more clip frames than it is
    applied over") and how M12's verification measured it (`age1cf.mjs`'s `gapOf`). The ratio of
    the media-time intervals is recorded beside it in each row (`rMedia`).
  - On `every`, `a = b = 1` and `M¹ · current` is the shipped prediction, so Fs calls the shipped
    step itself: the self-test asserts it. A media-time ratio would differ from 1 by the rounding
    in the clips' frame times, and Fs would not be the shipped step there.
  - `M^(b/a)` (`powH`): `M` conjugated into a frame centred on the image and scaled by its
    half-size, so that a motion of a few pixels is a matrix near the identity; its determinant set
    to 1; then `exp((b/a) · log M)`, by inverse scaling and squaring with Denman–Beavers square
    roots, and conjugated back. A square root that does not converge within its cap, or does not
    square back, and a logarithm that does not exponentiate back, make it `null`. The self-test
    checks `M¹ = M`, `M² = M·M`, two halves make `M`, a quarter of a translation is a quarter, and a
    half turn (no principal root) is refused.
  - **M12's verification scaled the velocity to first order,** `V_a = I + a(V − I)`
    (`age1cf.mjs`); `M^(b/a)` is the exact power, equal to it at `a = 1` and close to it for small
    motions. The registration chooses between them; only `M^(b/a)` is built here.
  - **Where `M^(b/a)` cannot be taken,** the row is **no verdict** (`noVerdict: true`,
    `eligible: false`, loss `fs-no-scaled-prediction`): never discordant, never carried, and counted
    apart in the summary (`fsNoScaledPrediction`). An instrument's failure is not the lever's
    refusal.
- **Ffb, at age 1 only:** the shipped step; on a refusal only, F0's step on the same frame. The
  second alignment is `extraSteps: 1`, charged one more `stepMs` on a device schedule (as #273's
  R3 is), nothing on `every`.
- **Every other step is the shipped step.**
- **A paired event:** a distinct lock state for a lever, within a clip and schedule. The key holds
  everything the lever's step depends on: the lever, the clip frame, `previous` and `current` bit
  for bit, V's chain `H` and Fs's `a` and `b`. A row repeating a state (the second loop; seeds that
  reach the same state) is kept, marked `repeats: true` with the event it repeats, and not counted
  or carried again. **Every repeat is checked, fatally, to give its event's results** (both arms'
  verdicts, reasons and `H`, and the charge): a key missing something the step depends on fails
  there.
- **Across a loop wrap:** every paired row, every `deep` row's paired row, every `m9` state and
  lever row, and every `wrong` row on the mirrored runs says whether its step crosses a loop wrap
  from the frame before (`acrossWrap`): the clip jumps back to its start between the lock's last
  frame and this one. Flagged, never dropped. On M9's exports every held step across a wrap is lost
  (40 on table, 22 on wall), and the milestone's held-step counts ("156 of about 2,652 and 53 of
  about 3,999") are exactly the counts without them; a registration decides whether they count.

## The modes

1. **`paired`:** for every lock state at a lever's age in the shipped arm's runs (clips and
   schedules given, seeds 1–8), the shipped step and the lever's on the same frame from the same
   state, through `trackFrame` as #273 calls it. Before any lever, the shipped step must reproduce
   the run's own step (verdict, inliers, fit RMS and `H`), and its reprojected corners the
   record's `corners`, fatally. One row per (clip, schedule, seed, record, lever): both verdicts
   with the refusal's reason, inliers, fit RMS, both outputs' corners, the reference beside each,
   the event, and `discordant` (`lever-kept`, `shipped-kept` or `null`).
2. **`deep`:** `paired`'s computation, and for every discordant event, in both directions, the
   arm that kept carried on from its own state (`previous` the paired step's `current`, `current`
   its output) with the shipped step, on the clip's frames as the schedule picks them: the next
   frame on `every`; on a device schedule `replay-run.mjs`'s rule (lines 115–117), the next frame
   after a kept step's busy time `DEVICE_ACQUIRE_MS[clip] + stepMs · (1 + extraSteps)`, the paired
   step's charge included. Each `deep` row holds the paired row it carries (`paired`), its outcome,
   `lasted`, `lost` (the step and its reason) or `censored` (`loop end`: the next frame is in the
   next loop or, in the run's last loop, past its last frame), every carried step with the
   reference beside it, and the lock's length.
   - **N** is `LASTING`, read from #273's analysis. Its unit is a lock's confirmed steps counted
     from its first step, so a lock reaching N lasts; a carry from a paired step at age `a` runs at
     most `N − (a + 1)` steps (`stepsToLast`, the conversion made once, with the reason beside
     it). Events censored before N are counted by reason in the summary; N is not changed for
     them.
   - **A carry of the shipped arm is checked against the run, fatally:** where the shipped step
     kept and the lever lost, the carry is the run itself going on, so each carried step must be
     the run's next record (its timeline index) and the run's own step there (verdict and `H`).
     This checks the carry's schedule and state against the replay; the negatives exercise it.
3. **`m9`:** every lock state of age 1 and later in the 16 exports, its step re-run on the
   desktop's decode of the same clip frame from the state rebuilt from the records.
   - **The placement is M12's verification's:** its scripts (`age1gaps.mjs`, `age1cf.mjs`) key the
     clip's frames by `Math.round(t · 1e6)`, which is `framesAt`'s `mediaKey`; they read the
     device's verdict as `trackLoss ? lost : kept`, which equals `state === "TRACK"` on all 8,092
     steps of the exports; and they select an age-1 state as a step whose record before is TRACK
     and the one before that DETECT, as the age here does. Both count every state, not events.
   - **The shipped step's reproduction first, by verdict class** (kept, lost). The measure: the
     verdict class must agree, **and, new in #278,** where both kept, the mean corner distance
     between the desktop's output corners and the record's `corners` must be at most
     `--repro-threshold`. M12's verification measured the verdict class only (its report, lines
     185–187); the summary prints that measure too, at age 1 by clip and class
     (`age1VerdictOnly`), with the states whose class differs (`age1VerdictDiffers`). On this
     machine its six counts equal the verification's on `nitro-walt`, as the two machines' decodes
     are identical (the figures are in the PR).
   - **Then F0, Fs and Ffb on the age-1 states that reproduce,** and on no other. Fs's `a` and
     `b` are read off the device's own processed frames. Ffb's second alignment is recorded, not
     charged: the device's frames are the device's.
4. **The reference beside every step** of `paired`, `deep` and `m9`, on the wall and table clips
   (#277's files, refused unless their bytes are as #277 recorded and they were made from this
   clip's bytes): the mean corner distance of the arm's output corners to the reference's on that
   clip frame, or `null` with the reason (the clip has no reference; the reference frame is
   rejected; the arm refused; the output has no corners), and `beyondMargin` against
   `--gross-margin`. The summaries count rows on a frame the reference accepts and rows on one it
   does not, whatever the arms did.
5. **`wrong`:** wrong acceptances, read on the step's output `H` (M11's ruling):
   - **On #273's synthetic sequences** (its truth script's eleven, listed in the script as named
     there, since that script runs on import): each run by the ground truth's own `run`, its states
     rebuilt from its results and the shipped step checked to reproduce each, fatally. "Wrong" is
     #273's own definition: a confirmed step whose output is more than `RIGHT` = 2 px RMS off the
     truth by `inView` (the analysis's `truthReport`; the truth script's header, "the first step's
     output `H` is scored the same way"). No parameter is needed.
   - **On #273's mirrored frames** (the runner's `runs --mirror --levers none`, every frame): every
     confirmed step is wrong, no threshold. The frames are mirrored as the runner mirrors them,
     and the shipped step must reproduce the mirrored run's, fatally.
   - **Fs cannot respond in `wrong`:** the sequences are stepped every frame and the mirrored runs
     are `every` only, so `a = b = 1` and Fs is the shipped step on every state there. Its rows are
     written; a zero difference from them is void, not "no effect" (step-protocol.md).
   - Per lever and age: states, steps confirmed, and wrong ones among them, written as the last
     row of the output.

Every mode writes `<out>/<mode>.jsonl` (a header line, then the rows) and
`<out>/<mode>-summary.json` (end-to-end counts). The header names the script's last commit, the
runner's, the levers' and the analysis's, the head and whether the tree is clean, each clip's
SHA-256, the inputs read (the runs folder or the exports folder, and each file's SHA-256), the
parameters (the threshold, the margin, N and its source) and whether it is a trial (`--trial`:
`TRIAL, not for registration`). **A run that is not a trial refuses an uncommitted tree.**

## The parameters for #280's registration

None has a default; a mode that needs one refuses to run without it.

- **`--repro-threshold <px>`** (`m9`): how far the desktop's output corners may be from the
  device's for a kept state to count as reproduced. It decides which device states inform the
  levers, so it is fixed before their rows are read.
- **`--gross-margin <px>`** (`paired`, `deep`, `m9`): the reference distance beyond which a row is
  flagged. #277's 3.75 px is not reused: its record says the rule over-reads and that a later use
  fixes its own margin from the reference's measured error (`artoolkit-nft-bench`'s #11) or states
  why it cannot.
- **No wrong-`H` threshold:** #273 defines one (2 px RMS, above), cited, not set here.
- **N** is not a parameter: it is `LASTING`.

## The guards and the fatal checks

Each refuses, naming what is wrong, before any row is written; every mode writes only at its end,
after every guard and every fatal check has passed.

- **The seeds:** exactly 1 to 8, each once.
- **The reference:** present, its bytes as #277 recorded them, made from this clip's bytes, one row
  per clip frame at its media time.
- **A run's frames:** every record at a clip frame's media time; on `every`, exactly the clip's
  frames over the runner's loops, in order (#283's rule); not on the device schedules, whose
  frames follow the tracker's state (`replay-run.mjs`). And #277's run check: the `none` arm of
  this clip, seed and schedule, at the clip's size.
- **M9's recorded states:** every record placed on a clip frame (`framesAt`); the export's clip,
  size and `sync` path; 16 exports, 8 a clip.
- **A required parameter missing,** or not a non-negative number.
- **Fatal checks:** the shipped step reproducing the run's (desktop, mirrored, synthetic); a
  record's corners equal to the shipped `H`'s; the state rebuilt from the records; a held step
  following a kept one; a lock started by a detection; a repeat giving its event's results; a carry
  of the shipped arm being the run.

**`--self-test`** builds its own inputs; it needs no build and no clip. It has a case each way for
each guard the brief lists (the seeds, a missing reference and one made from another clip, a
record off the clip's frames, an `every` run of other frames, an unplaced M9 state, a missing
parameter), and for the reference's hash and row branches and a non-numeric parameter; and for:
Fs as the shipped step on `every` and a scaled prediction elsewhere; `M^r`, and no verdict where it
cannot be taken; Ffb falling back and charged a second step on a device schedule, none on
`every`; a discordant event carried N steps, lost before N, and censored at a loop's end and at the
last loop's; a device carry on `replay-run.mjs`'s timing; a step across a wrap, both ways; a
repeated state marked as repeating, and a repeat with other results refused; a recorded state
beyond the threshold or of another verdict class reported as not reproduced and given no lever
rows, and one within it given F0, Fs and Ffb.

## How to run each mode

From the repository root, after `npm run build`; ffmpeg and ffprobe on `PATH`.

```
# the shipped arm's runs, by #273's runner, into <runs>
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-14-272-stage1-probe.mjs runs --out <runs> --schedule <every|device-15|device-25> --clips table,wall --seeds 1-8 --levers none
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-14-272-stage1-probe.mjs runs --out <runs> --schedule every --clips table,wall --seeds 1-8 --levers none --mirror

node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs --self-test
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs paired --runs <runs> --out <out> --gross-margin <px> [--clips table,wall] [--schedules every,device-15,device-25]
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs deep --runs <runs> --out <out> --gross-margin <px> [--clips …] [--schedules …]
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs m9 --out <out> --repro-threshold <px> --gross-margin <px>
node --experimental-transform-types docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-tools.mjs wrong --runs <runs> --out <out>
node docs/benchmarks/2026-11-22-278-stage1-negatives.mjs --runs <runs> --work <dir outside the repository>
```

`--clips` takes any of `static`, `wall`, `table` and `cross`; only wall and table have a reference.
`--seeds` defaults to `1-8`, the only set the guard admits. `--references <dir>` and
`--exports <dir>` read #277's files and M9's exports from another folder (the negatives use them).
A new clip (#279's) needs its entry in #262's `CLIP_FILES`, the runner's `DEVICE_ACQUIRE_MS` and,
for the reference, #277's `CLIPS`.

## Limits

- **The prototypes are #273's and this script's,** not the tracker's: each lever acts on one step
  from a recorded state, and `deep` carries with the shipped `trackFrame`, not the tracker's state
  machine. A carried lock ends at its first refusal; what the tracker would do next (detect again)
  is not followed.
- **V's eligibility is #273's chain:** a detection on the processed frame before. A velocity from
  older detections, or scaled like Fs, is not built.
- **`m9` re-runs the step on the desktop's decode,** not the device's. Whether the two decodes
  agree is what the reproduction threshold stands in for.
- **No ground truth on the clips:** the reference informs (is the output on the poster?); it does
  not judge a few pixels.
