# #228, Stage 1 repeated on the three tilt clips: IPPE against the closed form above 10°, the guard on one crossing, and the assumed `K` (2026-11-06)

**A Stage 1 probe under [the step protocol](./step-protocol.md), run on 2026-11-06: a desktop
replay, headless, of the three tilt clips added in #232, RANSAC seeded per frame, seeds 1 to 8. Not
campaign data; never reportable as a round. It measures and registers nothing: no criterion, no
prediction, no change to `METRICS_VERSION` or under `packages/*/src`. The tilt clips have no device
acquisition constant, so the constant on the two device schedules is borrowed from the static clip
(36.9 ms), not measured, and every figure on them is labelled. The chessboard calibration in question
E is maintainer-side and is not in this repository. No Stage 2 or Stage 3 text is drafted here: the
next step is the joint document of the rulings' item 6, from the maintainer's brief.**

## E. The assumed `K` against the guard's threshold

**The question:** the threshold of 10° comes from a construction that renders and estimates with the same `K`, while the guard reads tilt through an assumed one. Does the crossover move when the true focal length differs?

**The construction.** The characterisation's item 2 and its extension, `test/pose/c4_characterisation.test.ts`: 13 tilts × 4 azimuths above 0° × 2 rolls × 2 shifts × 3 seeds, 588 fits at 270 × 360. `rigidView` and `renderWarp` are imported from that test's TypeScript through esbuild, and its `fit` is copied, so the assumed row is the proof.
