## The method

The probe follows #137's pipeline probe
([`2026-10-28-137-stage1-pipeline-probe.md`](./2026-10-28-137-stage1-pipeline-probe.md),
#189) in form; the script's header carries the method in full.

- **Decoding and schedule**, copied from `scripts/replay-clips.mjs` at
  `9b387ae` (`probe`, `greyFrames`, `loadClip`, lines 441 to 508;
  `loopPeriod`, `loopTimes`, lines 542 to 552): six loops, every frame in
  order, loops 1 to 4 counted. The file changed at `4d785a9` (#207) since
  #137's copy, so the copy was re-proved, not assumed (check D).
- **The wrapper:** `estimateHomography` gets `rng` (one mulberry32 stream a
  run, seeds 1 to 8). On an accepted fit it reads both axes; with at least
  5 inliers it applies the run's candidate. **A fit with fewer than 5
  inliers is passed through untouched:** `detection.ts` refuses it at line
  455, so it is G5's refusal, not the candidate's, and it is never counted
  as a candidate refusal. A candidate's refusal returns the backend's own
  failure shape, as in #137's probe.
- **Axis A, the side, from `H` alone** (the ruling):
  `wᵢ = H[6]·xᵢ + H[7]·yᵢ + H[8]` on the 25 points of
  `targetGrid(level-0 size)`. All of one sign and none zero: `s` is that
  sign, and the frame is `facing` when `s · det H > 0` (the determinant of
  `s·H`, whose `w` are positive, has the sign of `s³ · det H`), `reversed`
  when `< 0`. Otherwise `mixed-w`, unscaled. `det H = 0` with one-signed `w`
  is not in the ruling and is fatal; it occurred nowhere.
- **Axis B, the depth, in `selectPose`'s own terms:** `degenerate` when
  `candidates` is null, else `two`, `one` or `none in front` for `rejected`
  0, 1 or 2. "In front" there is `inFront` on the grid **and** a finite RMS
  (`select_pose.ts`, lines 163 to 164); a candidate with a zero-depth
  correspondence is rejected without failing the depth test. The probe
  counts the frames where `rejected` differs from the candidates' own
  `inFront`: 0 on every clip, every run, all six loops.
- **Beside the axes, on each DETECT frame:** the outcome and `chosen`;
  whether the selected pose passes `inFront` (#137's "behind the camera");
  and, for reconciliation with #202's body only, whether the selected pose's
  `R[8] < 0`. That last is #202's reading, not the ruling's side.
- **The tracker's `H` on a DETECT frame is the fit's**, element for element,
  and its selection's outcome and `chosen` equal the wrapper's recomputation
  (fatal checks): the axes are read on exactly the fit the tracker posed.
- **Tables** are over the counted loops' DETECT frames; the algebra and the
  mirrored-corners check cover every DETECT frame of all six loops.

## The classes and the algebra's check

**The cross-table is disjoint and exhaustive by construction**, each axis
being so, and shown: the twelve cells of every run sum to its DETECT count
(fatal check).

**The algebra.** On an exact decomposition `H = λ·K·[r₁ r₂ t]`, the grid's
`w = λ·z`, so with `H` scaled to `w > 0`, `sign det H = sign(r₃·t) ·
sign(z)`. The brief's equivalence holds for a pose whose grid is in front
and flips for one whose grid is behind. The ruled check compares, in
columns `facing` and `reversed`, `sign(r₃·t)` (`r₃ = (R[2], R[5], R[8])`)
of both IPPE candidates and the closed form against the column, fatal at
the first disagreement. Column `mixed-w` is excluded: the side is not
defined there, and no scaling makes the `w` one-signed.

- **Result: no disagreement on any DETECT frame** of all six loops, any
  clip, candidate or seed.
- **Its coverage on the interesting population is near zero, and the record
  says so.** In columns `facing` and `reversed`, every DETECT frame on the
  corpus is in row `two in front` except three `facing × one` frames on the
  table clip (s3 one, s7 two); no frame there has `none in front`. Only two
  poses with `inFront` false reached the check: the closed form on the table
  clip's seed 5, frames 8 and 809 (`reversed × two`, ambiguous, the fallback
  selected; post-run read of the per-frame rows), where the grid crosses
  z = 0 rather than lying behind; both agreed. The advisor's first
  consultation predicted that the check would trip on poses with the grid
  behind; it did not, because on this corpus such poses occur only in
  column `mixed-w`, which the check excludes.
- **Mirrored corners:** the signed area of the four projected target corners
  against theirs in target coordinates equals column `reversed` on every
  DETECT frame outside `mixed-w` (fatal check). The corners are grid points
  because the target's meta size equals its level-0 size, 512 × 640
  (checked).
- **The convention's anchor:** the static clip's frame 0, the one detection
  both probes take as right (G0, seed 1, 114 inliers), reads `facing`,
  `two in front`, `selected`, and `sign(r₃·t) = +1` on both candidates and
  the closed form (fatal if not).
