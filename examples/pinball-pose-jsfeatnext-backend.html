CvBackend — pinball pose demo

NftTracker on targets/pinball.wnft, with its pose drawn through K · [R | t]: the target's outline, a box standing on it and its three axes, beside the corners under H that the other demos draw. The pose filter (poseFilter, One Euro, off by default) changes no pixel of those corners; here it shows. With the unfiltered pose (poseSelection.pose) drawn too, the two coincide with the filter off and show its effect side by side with it on. A display, not a measurement: this page exports nothing (see examples/README.md).
